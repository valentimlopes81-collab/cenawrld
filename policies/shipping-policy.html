How it works

As soon as you place your order you will receive a confirmation e-mail and our team gets to work so your order reaches you as soon as possible.

  • If you have not received your order within the delivery time listed below for your country, contact us with your order number and we will find a solution as soon as possible.
  • Cena is not responsible for delivery mistakes caused by an incorrect address entered at checkout.
  • During drops, Black Friday and Christmas, high demand may cause some delays.
  • To cancel an order, e-mail cenahelping@gmail.com. Cancellations are only possible for unfulfilled orders.

Good to know

  • Transit times are approximate and may vary. They are not exact delivery dates.
  • If an order has to be cancelled for a reason beyond our control, we will let you know within 24 working hours and refund you using the payment method you used.
