trouble always finds us. we felt out of place, disconnected. nobody ever understood our goals and our dreams, they called them unrealistic. nobody ever had our vision.

so we took refuge in the effort, in the hustle, in fashion, in art. we've always been addicted to creating, addicted to sweating, addicted to overcoming. we stayed loyal to those who believed in our vision. we always wanted something more.
 

today we tell the story without words. each piece is a page left unwritten, a part of the story we want to tell.

today the vehicle of the mind is the touch of the fabric, the cut, the design, the process. feeding the streets it's all about giving back to those who feel us and connect with our beliefs, and we manifest a movement that carries the values of the street and resilience in its veins, far beyond clothing.
